## About

I'm a software engineer based in Sousse, Tunisia, working across product development, cybersecurity, cloud and AI.

Founder and CEO of [TuniCyberLabs](https://tunicyberlabs.com). Co-founder and tech lead at [DIGISUBS AGENCY](https://digisubs.online), [BreakNRoot](https://breaknroot.tn) and [Innovation T](https://innovation-t.tn).

Previously, I spent two years as a full-stack developer at [DuetoData](https://duetodata.com).
